**Project title:** Lamp: Bible in a Year (Android app)

**Publisher / developer:** Angelica Quebec (independent developer), Cavite, Philippines

**Format:** Free mobile app on Google Play (Android). An iOS version may follow.

**Price / monetization:** Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.

**Description of use:**
Lamp is a one-year Bible reading plan app. Each day it shows four short portions (Old Testament, New Testament, Psalms, Proverbs), so users read the whole Bible in a year. The app does not bundle or store the NLT text in its package. Passages are fetched on demand from Tyndale's official NLT API (api.nlt.to) when a user opens a reading. Fetched passages are cached on the user's own device for offline reading. The full NLT copyright notice appears below every passage:

> Scripture quotations are taken from the Holy Bible, New Living Translation, copyright © 1996, 2004, 2015 by Tyndale House Foundation. Used by permission of Tyndale House Publishers, Carol Stream, Illinois 60188. All rights reserved.

**Amount of NLT text:** Over a year, a user reads the entire Bible through the API, one daily portion at a time (about 115 verses a day).

**Other translations:** The public-domain World English Bible (WEB) is bundled as an offline fallback.

**Expected audience / distribution:** Personal and church community use at first, publicly available on Google Play. Expected installs: under 1,000 in the first year.
