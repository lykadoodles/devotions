**Project title:** Lamp: Bible in a Year (Android app, package `com.lykadoodles.lamp`)

**Publisher / developer:** Angelica Quebec (independent developer), Cavite, Philippines

**Contact:** aql.ai.labs@gmail.com · https://lykadoodles.github.io/devotions/

**Format:** Free mobile app on Google Play (Android). Currently in closed testing with about 16 testers from our
church community; public release planned after testing. An iOS version may follow.

**Price / monetization:** Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no account, no data collection.

**Description of use:**
Lamp is a one-year Bible reading plan with a guided daily devotion: an opening prayer, four short readings
(Old Testament, New Testament, Psalms, Proverbs), a key takeaway, and a closing prayer. Readers go through the
whole Bible in a year.

How NLT text is used:
- **Not bundled.** The app package contains no NLT text. Passages are fetched on demand from Tyndale's official
  NLT API (api.nlt.to) when a reader opens a reading, using an API key registered for the app.
- **Saved on the reader's own phone** after first download, so a passage isn't requested again and can be read
  offline. Nothing is uploaded or shared elsewhere.
- **Read aloud (optional):** the reader can have the passage on screen read aloud by their phone's built-in
  text-to-speech voice. No recordings are made or distributed.
- **Verse of the day (optional):** a single verse shown in the app and in a daily phone notification.
- **Sharing:** a reader can copy or share a verse they selected, with the reference and "NLT".
- The full NLT copyright notice appears below every passage:

> Scripture quotations are taken from the Holy Bible, New Living Translation, copyright © 1996, 2004, 2015 by
> Tyndale House Foundation. Used by permission of Tyndale House Publishers, Carol Stream, Illinois 60188.
> All rights reserved.

**Amount of NLT text:** One day's portions at a time (about 115 verses a day). Over a year, a reader goes through
the entire Bible.

**Other translations in the app:** Berean Standard Bible, KJV and World English Bible (public domain), Tagalog and
Cebuano Unlocked Literal Bibles (CC BY-SA), and ESV through Crossway's API.

**Expected audience / distribution:** Our church community first, then publicly on Google Play.
Expected: under 5,000 readers in the first year.

**Questions for Tyndale:**
1. Is a license or permission letter required to display NLT text through the NLT API in a free public app,
   including saving passages on the reader's phone for offline reading?
2. The shared API key allows 5,000 requests a day. Could Lamp have a dedicated key with a higher daily limit
   as readers grow?
3. Is reading the on-screen NLT text aloud with the phone's built-in voice acceptable?
4. Are there any additional attribution or display requirements?

Thank you for making the NLT available to developers. It's the translation our testers asked for most.
