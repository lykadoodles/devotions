# Permission request: Philippine Bible Society (Tagalog and Cebuano)

**Send to:** Philippine Bible Society, through the contact form at https://bible.org.ph or by phone at (632) 8526 7777.
Ask for the person who handles **copyright permissions / digital licensing**. The only email on their site,
app-feedback@bible.org.ph, is for their own app, so ask them which address to use.

**Subject:** Permission request: Magandang Balita Biblia and a Cebuano version in a free Bible-reading app

Dear Philippine Bible Society,

Good day! My name is Angelica Quebec, an independent developer from Cavite. I've built **Lamp: Bible in a Year**,
a free Android app (Google Play, currently in closed testing) that helps people read the whole Bible in one year
through a guided daily devotion: an opening prayer, four short readings (Old Testament, New Testament, Psalms and
Proverbs), a key takeaway, and a closing prayer.

Our testers, mostly from our church community, have asked to read in **Tagalog and Cebuano**. The app currently
offers the Tagalog and Cebuano Unlocked Literal Bibles (CC BY-SA), but many readers know and love the Philippine
Bible Society's translations. I'd like to request permission to include:

1. **Magandang Balita Biblia** (Tagalog Popular Version)
2. A modern **Cebuano** version from PBS (for example, *Ang Bag-ong Maayong Balita Biblia*), whichever you recommend

**About the app**
- Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no account required
- Shows one day's readings at a time (about 115 verses a day); readers go through the whole Bible over a year
- Reading progress stays on the reader's phone; we collect no personal data
- The full copyright notice you specify would appear under every passage
- Text would be shown unchanged
- Expected reach: our church community first, then publicly on Google Play (under 5,000 readers in the first year)

**Questions**
1. Is permission possible for a free app like this, and what are the terms (fees, attribution, limits)?
2. Can the text be stored on the phone for offline reading, or do you offer an API or a licensing partner instead?
3. Is there a required copyright line or logo?

Thank you for your ministry in bringing God's Word to Filipinos in their own languages. I'd be grateful for any
guidance, and I'm happy to send screenshots or a test version of the app.

God bless,
