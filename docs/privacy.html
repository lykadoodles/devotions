Lamp: Bible in a Year

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Lamp is a Bible reading app. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name (if you enter one), reading plan start date, reading progress, streaks, badges and settings.
  • Your prayer list, answered-prayer notes, highlighted verses and journal notes.
  • Bible passages you have opened, saved so you can read them offline.

This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The optional "Back up progress" feature creates a code that you copy and keep yourself; we never receive it.

Internet use

To show the New Living Translation (NLT) or the English Standard Version (ESV), the app requests Bible passages from the publishers' official services: Tyndale House Publishers' NLT API (api.nlt.to) and Crossway's ESV API (api.esv.org). Each request contains only the passage reference (for example, "John 3") and an API key. No personal information is included. Each publisher's handling of these requests is governed by its own privacy policy. The Berean Standard Bible, King James Version and World English Bible are built into the app and need no internet connection.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download NLT or ESV passages.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminder after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for gentle feedback when you finish a reading.

Children

Lamp is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
