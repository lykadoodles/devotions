Lamp: Bible in a Year

“Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path.” (Psalm 119:105)

Read the whole Bible in a year with four short portions a day: Old Testament, New Testament, Psalms and Proverbs. That's about 15 minutes a day, and you read Proverbs twelve times a year. Streaks, a growing flame, a bookshelf that fills as you go, badges and gentle daily reminders help you keep going.
