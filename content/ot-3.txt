362|"Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit." Do not despise small beginnings.|Do one small thing for God today without despising it.|Lord, not by my might, but by your Spirit.
363|God will dwell in Jerusalem again. Your King comes, righteous and humble, riding on a donkey.|Welcome Jesus as your humble King today.|Jesus, my humble King, I welcome you.
364|They will look on the one they pierced. A fountain will cleanse sin. The Lord will be King over all.|Look to Jesus, the one who was pierced for you.|Lord, open the fountain that cleanses my sin. Be King over all.
365|God loves His people and calls for wholehearted worship. "Bring the whole tithe... and see if I will not open the windows of heaven." The sun of righteousness will rise.|Give God your best and trust Him to provide.|Father, I bring you my whole heart. Let the sun of righteousness rise with healing.
